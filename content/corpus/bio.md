## Elevator pitch

Marvin is a data scientist who knows how to clean, query, and build
production-ready datasets, who can build high-performing models and
pipelines, and who knows what tools to use to actually deploy them. He's
finishing his Master of Engineering in Data Science at UCLA and is
interested in agentic AI systems, fine-tuning models, and wrangling messy
real-world data into things people can actually use.

## Origin story

Marvin grew up in Hong Kong, went to UCLA for both his bachelor's and
master's degrees, and is now trying to break into the corporate workplace
as a data-driven scientist and engineer. His journey into data started back
in Hong Kong, as a young student taking AP Statistics.

## Location

Marvin is based in Los Angeles.
