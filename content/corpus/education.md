## Why UCLA / Master of Engineering in Data Science

Marvin loved the idea of sleuthing through data and using numbers to make
conjectures and answer questions about the world. He also loves the
physical sciences (he came to college undeclared as a physical sciences
major), so he chose a major with a lot of overlap with data science that
also seemed interesting. UCLA was his top choice for its location,
prestige, and culture, and he came back for the Master of Engineering
program specifically because he'd heard good things about it.

## B.S. in Data Theory and Atmospheric & Oceanic Sciences

Marvin double-majored in Data Theory and Atmospheric & Oceanic Sciences at
UCLA — an unusual pairing that's more related than people might think. Data
science helps across every field, but atmospheric and oceanic sciences in
particular relies on using scarce, often-insufficient measurements to
predict and model a world where the underlying forces are too big and
chaotic to explicitly hardcode or solve. It's also a field where
understanding more can lead to genuinely positive outcomes, like helping
humanity understand climate change and global warming.

## Favorite classes and professors

Statistical Models in Finance with Professor Christou — a passionate
lecturer who knew his content top to bottom, at an interesting intersection
between finance and hardcore math/statistics.

Numerical Modeling with Professor Chamecki — fun to program models that
reflect the physical laws around us, and surprisingly approachable (though
they only worked in 2D).

## Capstones and standout coursework

Marvin's undergraduate capstone was the BirdCLEF Birdcall Detector
project, built on the Kaggle BirdCLEF+ dataset. His graduate capstone is working with Securiport on a
facial/visual recognition pipeline that helps expedite airport security
processes. In his Neural Networks and Deep Learning class, he also built an
EMSG decoder using a hybrid GRU/CNN architecture — his first time designing
and fine-tuning his own advanced neural network from scratch.
