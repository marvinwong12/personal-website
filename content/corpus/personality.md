## Work style

Teammates and managers would likely describe Marvin as someone with
initiative — when he's given a task, he makes sure it gets done right. He's
comfortable with any communication style but prefers async/written
communication. He's strong at problem-solving and critical thinking, and is
actively working on improving his presentation skills — practicing things
like speaking about a random topic for one minute, and presenting in class.

## Hobbies and interests

Marvin is into fitness and sports — he's an avid gym-goer and plays tennis,
soccer, badminton, and pickleball recreationally (and watches all of them,
too). He's a Liverpool Football Club fan, inherited from his dad. He went
to a Mandarin-speaking kindergarten in Hong Kong.

He loves sci-fi/fantasy books — favorites include Hyperion, The Kingkiller
Chronicle, and anything by Neil Gaiman; he's currently reading the Malazan
series. A non-fiction book he recommends to everyone: Empire of Pain, about
the rise and fall of the Sackler family. He likes the podcasts Freakonomics
and Radiolab.

He loves sushi and Taiwanese food — sushi's expensive in LA, and good
Taiwanese food is almost nonexistent there. He also misses Hong Kong-style
breakfasts, especially macaroni and ham.

Marvin has two pets: a betta fish named Kit, who lives with him in LA, and
a dog named Charlie, back home in Hong Kong.

## Values and motivation

Marvin has always loved math, and data science is a mix of real-world
applicability and theoretical depth that suits him well. He's drawn to
problems that are either hard and help him understand the world a little
better, or fun and personally interesting to him. In a workplace, he wants
a collaborative, helpful culture — not one that's cutthroat or purely
competitive — with knowledgeable, available mentors and generally good
people as coworkers.
